Cooley 在 1902 年提出「镜中我」:我们通过想象别人怎么看自己,来形成对自己的看法。三步——我想象你怎么看我,我想象你如何评价我,我因此感到羞耻或者骄傲。

问题出在第二步:那一步永远是猜的。

我发过一条朋友圈,写的时候在脑子里排演了三种读者的反应,删改四次。发出去以后,点赞的有两个。我预演的那三种反应,一种也没发生。

后来我还是会预演。只是会提醒自己一句:剧本是我写的,观众没看过。

---

**The Looking-Glass Self**

Cooley proposed the looking-glass self in 1902: we come to see ourselves through imagining how others see us. Three steps — I imagine how you see me, I imagine how you judge me, and I feel shame or pride accordingly.

The trouble is step two. It is always a guess.

I once wrote a post and rehearsed three different readers' reactions in my head, revising four times. Two people liked it. None of the three reactions I had rehearsed happened.

I still rehearse. I just remind myself: I wrote the script; the audience never read it.
