去年我删掉了一个文件夹,以为里面是导出的中间文件。三天后才发现,那是唯一一份原始素材。

那次之后我开始用 restic。它的快照按内容寻址,只要文件还被某个快照引用着,就能捞回来。

但保留策略是我自己定的:7 个日备、4 个周备、6 个备用月备。也就是说,半年之后,那份素材还是会彻底消失。

备份没有让时间停下来。它只是把「彻底失去」这件事,往后推了六个月。

---

**Six Months**

Last year I deleted a folder I thought was exported intermediates. Three days later I realised it was the only copy of the source material.

That's when I started using restic. Its snapshots are content-addressed, so anything still referenced by one can be pulled back.

But the retention policy is mine: seven daily, four weekly, six monthly. Which means in six months, that material disappears for good.

Backup doesn't stop time. It just pushes the moment of total loss six months further out.
