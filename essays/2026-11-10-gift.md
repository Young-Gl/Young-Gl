有次朋友请我吃饭,我认真说了句谢谢,回家路上越想越不对劲。

Mauss 在 1925 年的《礼物》里讲,礼物从来不是免费的。它带着三种义务:给、收、还。拒绝回礼,等于拒绝维持这段关系——不是礼貌问题,是关系要不要继续的问题。

所以「谢谢」不够。谢谢是语言的,回礼是行动的。

我后来买了两瓶酒送过去。他什么也没说,下次还是他请。

这件事我到现在也没想明白:到底是我还了,还是我又欠了一笔。

---

**The Gift**

A friend took me to dinner once. I thanked him properly, then spent the walk home feeling that it wasn't enough.

In *The Gift* (1925), Mauss argued that a gift is never free. It carries three obligations — to give, to receive, to return. Refusing to return is not rudeness; it is a refusal to continue the relationship.

So "thank you" doesn't cover it. Thanks is language. Returning is action.

I bought him two bottles of wine. He said nothing, and paid again the next time.

I still haven't worked out whether I repaid him or just incurred a second debt.
