我在这个小区住了三年。我知道外卖平台上有二十七家店能在四十分钟内送到,但不知道楼下那家面馆几点关门。

项飙把这种现象叫作「附近的消失」:人能够理解很大的世界(通过手机),也能理解很小的家庭,但中间那一层——邻里、街角、菜市场——正在变薄。

他讲的不是怀旧。附近消失意味着一种能力的退化:处理具体的、面对面的、无法退出的人际关系的能力。在网上,你可以不看、不回复、拉黑;在楼下不行。

我试着去那家面馆吃了两次。老板现在认识我了。但我还是不知道他几点关门。

**The Neighbourhood**

I have lived in this compound for three years. I know twenty-seven restaurants that can reach me in forty minutes. I don't know what time the noodle shop downstairs closes.

Xiang Biao calls this the disappearance of the nearby: people can grasp the very large (through a phone) and the very small (their own family), while the layer in between — neighbours, street corners, the market — thins out.

He isn't being nostalgic. What thins out is a capacity: dealing with specific, face-to-face relationships you can't log out of. Online you can ignore, not reply, block. Downstairs you can't.

I went and ate there twice. The owner knows me now. I still don't know when he closes.
