有人问我为什么不做账号体系。我说不是出于信念,是懒。

有账号就得有服务器、有数据库、有找回密码的流程,还得负责它别泄露。没有账号,这些问题一个都不存在。

代价也很实在:我不知道有多少人在用,不知道他们卡在哪一步,也没法看留存。这些数据对做产品的人有用,我只是选择不要。

Ivan Illich 说工具应该扩大人的自主。我不确定一个菜单栏小工具能承载这么大的词,但至少它可以做到一件事:你删掉它的时候,不用跟谁交代。

---

**What I Don't Build**

Someone asked why there's no account system. I said laziness, not principle.

An account means a server, a database, a password reset flow, and the responsibility when it leaks. Without accounts, none of those exist.

The cost is real too: I don't know how many people use it, where they get stuck, or whether they come back. That data is useful. I just chose not to have it.

Illich wrote that tools should enlarge autonomy. I'm not sure a menu bar utility can carry a word that big. But it can do one thing — when you delete it, you don't owe anyone an explanation.
