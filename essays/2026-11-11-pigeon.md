---
title: 今天我是一只鸽子 · Today I Am a Pigeon
date: 2026-11-11
summary: 自由的意思是没人管我,也没人找我。
---

今天我是一只鸽子  
这活没编制  
六点在晾衣杆打卡  
灰的,红脚爪  
算制服  

我年轻时候想当鹰  
那边名额满了  
就来了这儿  

早市我在豆腐车后头  
吃地上的米粒  
挑干净的  
不干净的不吃  

一个老人弯腰捡葱  
弯得比我低头还慢  
我们互相点头  
像两个各有难处的同事  

去年冬天少了两只  
没人报失  
广场上照旧有人撒食  
食照旧不够  

天黑往回飞  
比电梯稳  

晾衣杆还横在那儿  
明天上不上工  
看天气  
看心情  

我们这行  
自由  
也没人找我

**Today I Am a Pigeon**

Today I am a pigeon  
no establishment post  
clock in at six on the clothesline  
grey, red feet  
that's the uniform  

When I was young I wanted to be a hawk  
they were full up on that side  
so I came here  

At the morning market I follow the tofu cart  
eat the rice off the ground  
the clean grains  
not the dirty ones  

An old man bends to pick up a scallion  
bends slower than I lower my head  
we nod to each other  
like two colleagues with different troubles  

Two went missing last winter  
nobody filed a report  
someone still scatters feed in the square  
still not enough  

Dusk, I fly back up  
steadier than the lift  

The clothesline is still across there  
whether I clock in tomorrow  
depends on the weather  
and the mood  

This trade  
is freedom  
and nobody looking for me
